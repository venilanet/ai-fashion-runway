Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Current state: demo preview

During this demo phase, everything runs in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and no video is generated. There is nothing to delete because nothing is stored.

2. What happens when live generation launches

  • Your photo is sent to our video-generation provider (fal.ai, which runs the video model) solely to create your runway video. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or model training by us.
  • Your video is generated on fal.ai's infrastructure and returned to you for download. We do not publish it anywhere.
  • Retention: uploaded photos and generated videos are deleted from our systems within 30 days, and from the provider's systems per their data-processing terms. You can request earlier deletion at any time (see §4).

3. What we do not collect

No accounts, no analytics cookies, no device fingerprinting in this build. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

4. Your rights

You can request a copy or deletion of anything we hold about you. The fastest way is via our contact page.

5. Children

This tool is not intended for anyone under 18.
