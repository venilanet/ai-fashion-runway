Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. What we process and why

  • Your photo (free browsing) — never leaves your device. Uploading a photo and switching templates on the public site are handled entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Your photo (paid generation) — when you purchase a video, your photo is sent to our video-generation provider (fal.ai, which runs the video model) for the sole purpose of creating your video. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or model training by us.
  • Your video — generated on the provider's infrastructure and returned to you for download. We do not publish it anywhere.
  • Waitlist email — if you join the waitlist, we store your email in our Cloudflare KV database so we can notify you at launch. Support is handled by email; we don't store support messages on our servers.
  • Analytics — we use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage (pages viewed, buttons clicked, early-access signups). It receives no photos, emails, or other personal data from us.
  • Payments — processed by our payment provider at public launch. We never see or store your full card number.

2. Retention & deletion

  • Uploaded photos and generated videos are deleted from our systems within 30 days, and from the provider's systems under their data-processing terms.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Support emails are handled in our mailbox and deleted on request.

You can request a copy or deletion of anything we hold about you at any time via the Support Center (choose "Privacy request").

3. What we don't do

No sale of personal data. No advertising trackers. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

4. Security

API keys and credentials live only in our server environment — never in frontend code. Support and waitlist data is stored in Cloudflare's infrastructure with access limited to operating the service.

5. Children

This service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

Material changes will be noted here with a new date. Continued use after changes means you accept them.
