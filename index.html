FAQ

What does AI Fashion Runway do?

AI Fashion Runway Generator turns one photo of you into a vertical AI runway video of you walking a couture runway — in one of three signature styles. You get an MP4 ready for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Can I make a full AI fashion show video?

Not yet — each video is a single AI runway video: one model, one walk, one of three signature styles. Video generation is currently in closed testing, so join the waitlist and we'll email you when it opens.

What photo should I upload?

A front-facing photo with your face uncovered and even lighting. Half-body or full-body shots give the most natural runway walk. Only upload your own photo or one you have permission to use.

What is Golden Sheer Wings?

Our signature style: enormous translucent golden silk wings — sheer flowing fabric with stage light shining through. They are not feather wings.

Will the video look exactly like me?

Close, not perfect. The AI keeps your face and hairstyle from the photo and animates a runway walk around them — a stylized version of you, not a replica.

What are the video specs?

We're targeting 13 seconds, vertical 9:16, 720p HD, MP4 download. Final specs are confirmed on our first live generations before public launch.

Who owns the video?

You do — including for commercial use. Your video is yours to post anywhere. Where platforms require it (e.g. TikTok's AI-generated content label), please tag it as AI-generated.

What can't I generate?

Photos of other people without their permission, group photos, photos of minors, and anything illegal, hateful or sexually explicit. Requests that break these rules — or our video provider's safety policies — are rejected before any charge.

What if my video fails to generate?

Failed generations are automatically re-credited — you never pay for a video you didn't get. If a payment issue comes up, see our Refund Policy or contact support.

Is this affiliated with a fashion brand?

No. Every scene, outfit and wing design is original. This is an independent AI tool, not endorsed by any fashion house or fashion show.
