FAQ

What photo should I upload?

A front-facing photo with your face uncovered and even lighting. Half-body or full-body shots give the most natural runway walk.

What is Golden Sheer Wings?

Our signature scene: enormous translucent golden silk wings — sheer flowing fabric that billows like silk in the wind, with stage light shining through. They are not feather wings. Because translucent fabric is harder for video models to render cleanly, this template is still being tested for motion quality.

Will the video look exactly like me?

Close, not perfect. The AI keeps your face and hairstyle from the photo and animates a runway walk around them — think of it as a stylized version of you, not a replica.

What can't I upload?

Photos of other people without their permission, group photos (the model can't know who to keep), and anything offensive or illegal. Live generation includes automated moderation.

How long is the video?

13 seconds, vertical 9:16 at 720p — sized for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Is this affiliated with a fashion brand?

No. Every scene, outfit and wing design here is original. This is an independent AI tool and is not endorsed by any fashion house or fashion show.

Is live generation available now?

Not yet — this is a demo preview of the full experience. Live generation is being connected now; check back very soon.
