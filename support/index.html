If your video failed to generate

  1. Check your credits first — failed generations are re-credited automatically within minutes. If the credit is back, just try again.
  2. Try once more with a clearer front-facing photo; most failures come from heavy filters or obscured faces.
  3. Still stuck? Send the form above with topic "Generation failed" — include the approximate time it happened so we can look it up.

Refund requests

Read our Refund Policy first, then send the form above with topic "Refund request" and your order number. We reply within 2 business days.

Finding your order number

Your order number (format AFR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) is in your purchase confirmation email and on every receipt. It's the fastest way for us to locate your payment.
